# Research boundary

Medica Geometrica models. It does not diagnose. It does not treat.

## Established in this repository

- Distance, distortion, and coherence as mathematical quantities
- Coupled ODE-style dynamics with noise
- Counterfactual branches
- Monte Carlo sampling
- Recursive OODA updates
- SHA-256 receipts

## Research hypothesis

Geometric structure in longitudinal physiological state contains predictive information beyond the component measurements alone.

## Not established

- That minimizing distortion heals disease
- That simulated interventions predict treatment response
- That coherence is a recognized clinical biomarker
- That this software may be used as a medical device

## Clinical rule

Biopsy, licensed examination, and standard labs remain the decision path for a scalp lesion or suspected burn. This software may only log research-normalized observations after those clinical acts, if a researcher chooses to do so.
